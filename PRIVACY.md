## What we collect

**We don't run accounts or servers.** Your scores, streaks and settings are stored only on
your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

**Advertising (Google AdMob).** The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To
deliver and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on
iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device
and app information, and ad interaction data. On iOS, ads are personalized only if you
allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and
in US states with applicable privacy laws, you are asked for consent and can change your
choice any time in **Settings → Ad privacy choices**. See
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Purchases (RevenueCat, Apple, Google).** If you buy "Remove Ads", the purchase is
processed by the App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to verify it; RevenueCat
receives the purchase receipt and an anonymous app-generated user ID. We never receive
your payment details. See [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## What we don't do

We don't sell personal information, and we don't collect your name, email, contacts or
precise location.
